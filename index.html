Before You Proceed

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Objection!! decide?

An AI model examines both statements step by step, weighing context, fairness and real-world practicality, and commits to a clear ruling.

Is it really unbiased?

Both parties receive exactly equal weight. The court is instructed to disregard who writes more, who is more emotional and who speaks first. No AI is infallible, so treat the ruling as a fair, reasoned second opinion.

Is my data private?

Your statements are sent to an AI model solely to produce the verdict. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Can I add voice or screenshots?

Yes. Each party may dictate their statement by voice (browser support varies) and attach a screenshot or photo as evidence.

Is this legal advice?

No. Objection!! offers AI guidance for everyday disagreements and is not a substitute for a lawyer.
